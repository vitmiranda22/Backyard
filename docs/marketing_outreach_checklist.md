# Backyard — Marketing Outreach Checklist

**This is a manual action checklist, not something that runs itself.** Every
draft below is real, ready-to-use copy, but posting it to Product Hunt,
Reddit, or a directory, and sending the outreach email, are things only you
can do (they require your own accounts, and in Reddit's case, an account
with enough karma/history not to read as spam). Nothing here should be
automated or mass-posted.

All copy pulls from `docs/press_kit.md`. If you change the app's
positioning later, update that file first, then these drafts.

---

## Product Hunt launch

**Tagline** (max ~60 chars):
> Walking tours, narrated by AI, in five different moods

**Maker's first comment** (post this as the top comment on your own launch):
> Hey Product Hunt 👋
>
> I built Backyard because I kept walking past interesting-looking buildings
> with zero idea what they were. It uses your location to narrate real,
> fact-checked history and hidden stories about the exact block you're
> standing on, automatically, as you walk. No searching, no planning.
>
> You pick a mood before you start: Time Machine (what a spot looked like
> decades ago), Hidden City (secrets hiding in plain sight), Dark Side
> (unsolved mysteries and dark history), Behind the Scenes (film locations
> and celebrity history), or Unfiltered (raw, funny, opinionated, like
> walking with a local friend). Every walk gets saved to a journal, and you
> can discover routes other people have published too.
>
> It's free to use, with an optional premium tier for a few extra moods and
> voices. Would love to hear what you think, and happy to answer anything
> about how the narration works.

**Gallery images**: use the 7 screenshots in
`marketing/site/assets/screenshots/`, in this order for the strongest first
impression: `01_home.png`, `07_mood_picker.png`, `06_route_detail.png`,
`03_compass.png`, `04_map_pins.png`.

## Reddit

Self-promotion rules vary a lot by subreddit and change over time, check
each subreddit's current rules before posting (many require a minimum
account age/karma, or a specific self-promo day/thread). These drafts are
starting points, adjust tone to match the specific subreddit's culture.

### r/SideProject (indie-maker audience, self-promotion generally welcome)

> **Title:** I built an app that narrates real history as you walk, no
> planning required
>
> Wanted to share something I've been building: Backyard, an app that turns
> any walk into a guided tour. It uses your phone's location to figure out
> what street or landmark you're near, then plays real, fact-checked
> narration about it automatically, no need to look anything up or plan a
> route first.
>
> You pick a "mood" before you start (five options, from lighthearted local
> history to true crime/dark history), and every walk gets saved to a
> journal so you can revisit it later. There's also a discover feed of
> routes other people have published.
>
> It's on iOS, free to use with an optional premium tier. Happy to answer
> questions about how it works technically or otherwise, this is a solo
> project.

### r/ios (or r/apple, check current self-promo rules first)

> **Title:** Backyard, an app that automatically narrates real history as
> you walk (no need to keep the app open)
>
> Made this for iOS: it uses location to trigger real audio narration about
> whatever street/landmark you're near, automatically, so you're not
> staring at your phone the whole walk. Five different narration "moods" to
> pick from, plus a journal of past walks and routes shared by other users.
> Free to use, curious what this community thinks of the concept.

## Cold outreach email template

Use for travel/productivity/city-life newsletter writers and bloggers who
cover apps. Fill in `[Name]` and `[Publication]` before sending, and only
send to people whose past coverage actually fits (a "best new apps"
roundup, a city-guide newsletter, a productivity/travel blog).

> Subject: An app that narrates real history as you walk, thought of
> [Publication]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I've been following [Publication] and thought Backyard might be a fit for
> something you cover, it's an app that turns any walk into a guided tour.
> It uses your location to narrate real, fact-checked history about the
> exact block you're standing on, automatically, as you walk, no planning
> or searching required.
>
> There are five narration "moods" (from lighthearted local history to true
> crime/dark history), a journal of past walks, and a way to discover
> routes other users have published. It's free to use on iOS, with an
> optional premium tier.
>
> Happy to send an App Store link, screenshots, or a promo code if useful,
> whatever's easiest. No pressure either way, thanks for reading.
>
> [Your name]

Assets to attach or link: `marketing/site/press.html` (the full press kit),
or the individual files in `marketing/site/assets/`.
