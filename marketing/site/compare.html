Backyard vs. GPSmyCity, VoiceMap, and Detour

All four apps narrate real places for you to walk through. The difference is what you have to do before you start walking.

GPSmyCity, VoiceMap, and Detour are all real, well-established self-guided walking tour apps, and each is genuinely good at what it does. All three work the same basic way: you browse a catalog, pick one specific pre-recorded tour for a specific city, pay for it, and follow its fixed route. The tour only covers the streets it was written for.

Backyard skips that entirely. There's no catalog to browse and no tour to pick. Wherever you actually are, Backyard automatically narrates real, fact-checked history and hidden stories about that exact spot, in one of five moods you choose once, not per city. Walk somewhere none of these apps have ever written a tour for, and Backyard still works.

When a competitor might be the better pick

Backyard isn't the right fit for everyone. If you want one specific, professionally scripted tour written by a named local historian or journalist, VoiceMap's author-written tours go deeper on a single curated route than automatic narration can. If you're heading somewhere very off the beaten path, GPSmyCity's catalog of 1,800+ cities is hard to beat for sheer coverage. If cinematic audio production matters more to you than spontaneity, Detour leans into that.

Backyard is the better fit if you'd rather not plan anything at all: no browsing a catalog, no picking a city in advance, no paying per tour. You just walk, anywhere, and it works.
