EXAMPLE

Event Zones

Each event gets its own zone on the map, shown above as the shaded circle around a pin. Walk through one, and every story Backyard narrates there ties into that event, not the block's usual history, so a walk through a parade route or festival grounds is actually about the parade or festival.

Stories That Evolve Over Time

An event zone's stories change as the event itself unfolds, so walking through it before, during, and after tells a different part of the story each time.
